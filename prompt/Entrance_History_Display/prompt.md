### Text and spatial information: early engineering history

Sources: [IMG_2675](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/G entrance nearby/IMG_2675.HEIC).

IMG_2675 shows a wall-mounted history collage near the supplied entrance area, with sepia and black-and-white photographs on a pale teal and white background. Readable captions identify "The Duncan Sloss School of Engineering and Architecture, 1950", "Peel Engineering Laboratory, 1934", "Inside the newly-built Peel Engineering Laboratory, 1935", and machinery being loaded into the Peel Engineering Laboratory in 1934. Another caption states that all equipment in the Peel Engineering Laboratory was gone during World War II. Lower photographs are captioned "Hands-on problem solving", "Surveying, 1951" and "Open Day, circa 1965".

The same entrance history panel includes a caption describing war-time civil-engineering graduates with Prof. F.A. Redmond, then Dean, seated in the center, and another about his return to restart engineering before the war broke out. These identities are attributed to printed captions rather than inferred from faces. The displayed laboratory, building and student photographs are historical images within an exhibit and do not show the current entrance furniture or current occupants.

### Text and spatial information: buildings and departments

Sources: [IMG_2676](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/G entrance nearby/IMG_2676.HEIC).

IMG_2676 shows another part of the entrance history display. A newspaper reproduction headed "Student Extra" and "New department for HKU" is captioned "Establishment of Department of Computer Science, 1988". A large building photo is captioned "Haking Wong Building, 1983". Another caption says the Redmond Building opened in 1970 during Prof. H.C.H. Gurney's deanship of Engineering and was renamed "Yam Pak Building" in 1996. Additional captions identify students outside Ho Tung Engineering Workshop and "Soil Mechanic Laboratory, circa 1951".

The entrance display in IMG_2676 combines building photographs, an architectural illustration, laboratory scenes and smaller student images on a teal-and-white patterned background. These are exhibit captions and artwork; they do not independently verify the current use or condition of the pictured buildings.

People, equipment and buildings pictured within the collage must not be counted as people or objects currently present in the entrance area.

### Text and spatial information: Innovation Wing events and donations

Sources: [IMG_2680](../../image_source/Pictures_Hzx/G entrance nearby/IMG_2680.HEIC).

IMG_2680 shows a later-event entrance display with captions "Inaugural InnoSharing, 2021", "Engineering Inno Show, 2020", "Plaque Unveiling at Opening Ceremony for Tam Wing Fan Innovation Wing, 2020", and "InnoHub Kick-off Event, 2021". Names are drawn from the printed captions; no participant is identified from facial appearance.
